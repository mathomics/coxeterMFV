Changing the model

Edit Model pageChanges
Scenario & objectiveThe scenario, the objective and, for communities, SteadyCom. Published scenarios list their source, models, medium, bounds and results.
Species & abundanceThe abundance of each species, which sum to 1, and new species from files.
Shared mediumThe uptake limit of each metabolite, in mmol gDW⁻¹ h⁻¹. At 0, a metabolite comes only from secretion.
Reactions & boundsBounds, reaction and gene knockouts and the objective, with filters, undo and CSV import and export of bounds.
Biological modelNew metabolites and reactions, and COBRA JSON or SBML export.
Omics & expressionExpression or proteomics overlays, metatranscriptomics and GIMME. Gene values reach reactions as the minimum for AND and the maximum for OR.

The objective can be any reaction with any coefficient, maximized or minimized. A community can use equal growth or a weighted sum of biomass reactions.

Analyses

Run them from Simulate, and set them under Simulation settings.

AnalysisComputes
FBAFluxes that optimize the objective at steady state within the bounds.
pFBAThe least total absolute flux that keeps a fraction of the optimum, 100% by default.
FVAThe minimum and maximum flux of each reaction at the objective requirement. Needs FBA, pFBA or Loopless first.
pFVAThe same ranges at the least total flux of pFBA.
Loopless FBAFBA without internal cycles, as a slower mixed-integer problem.
CycleFreeFluxAn FBA solution without internal cycles, with the same objective and exchanges.
SteadyComOne growth rate, μ, for a whole community. Calculate range finds the feasible μ and Solve at this μ the abundances. Under Scenario & objective.
GIMMEFluxes that avoid reactions expressed below a threshold. Under Omics & expression.

FBA optima are rarely unique, so check internal fluxes with pFBA, CycleFreeFlux or FVA.

Reading the map

Cells and compartments

  • Each organism is a cell, whose name plate shows its growth rate, μ, in h⁻¹ after an analysis.
  • Compartments are nested as in the cell, and the Legend lists their colors. A dashed outline is a space without a membrane, such as the shared medium.
  • A reaction inside a compartment has all its metabolites there. Transport reactions sit on the membrane they cross, and exchange reactions on the cell boundary.
  • Cells of a community exchange metabolites through the shared medium.

Reaction lines

Color shows the size of the flux, from gray through blue and violet to red, on a compressed scale. The Legend gives its values in mmol gDW⁻¹ h⁻¹.

Width by flux widens lines with the flux.

Arrows point in the direction of the flux.

Gray dashed lines carry no flux.

Pink dashed lines are closed, with both bounds at 0.

Flux ranges

With FVA or pFVA, each reaction shows [minimum, maximum]. Each bound comes from its own optimization, so the bounds of different reactions need not occur in one solution. A single value means the flux is the same in every optimum.

Communities

  • A solution gives each cell's secretion and uptake, not who feeds whom. Cross-feeding lines split each metabolite among producers and consumers in proportion to them, with external supply as a producer and washout as a consumer.
  • The Cross-feeding explorer, in the Simulate tab, lists each transfer and chooses which appear on the map.
  • After SteadyCom, reaction lines are per gDW of that species, and cross-feeding lines per gDW of the community, abundance times flux.

Differences

In the phenylalanine rescue, Flux display under Scenario & objective shows Δ flux vs WT monoculture, blue for a decrease and red for an increase.

Reactions that are not drawn

Every analysis solves the whole model. In Reactions & bounds, Not on map lists the reactions not drawn, and Active those with flux.

Privacy

Calculations run in your browser, and nothing is uploaded. There is no login, cookie or tracking. The Yeast9 and phenylalanine rescue examples download public model files.

Common problems

ProblemCheck
No feasible solutionMedium, bounds, objective, knockouts and maintenance, and for communities the abundances.
Internal fluxes differ between runsAlternative optima. Compare objective values, or use pFBA or FVA.
FVA or pFVA unavailableRun FBA, pFBA or Loopless first.
Flux in a reaction not on the mapFind it under Not on map, and add it from the Reaction library.
An example does not loadYeast9 and the phenylalanine rescue need internet access.
Anything elseReport it on GitHub issues, without unpublished data.
