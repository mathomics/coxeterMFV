Coxeter Multicell Flux Visualizer

Coxeter-MFV

Metabolic maps of cells and microbial communities, with compartments nested as in the cell, transport on the membranes it crosses and organisms joined through a shared medium. Solve the model with FBA, pFBA, FVA, loopless FBA or SteadyCom and see the fluxes on the same map.

Open the app Help Free, no login. Runs in your browser, so your models stay on your computer.

What you can do

Use your own model

Import SBML Level 3 with FBC, COBRA JSON, COBRA Toolbox MAT or Excel. Several models make a community. Formats and sample files.

Draw cells and compartments

Each reaction sits in its compartment, and each transport reaction on the membrane it crosses. Small models are laid out for you.

Analyze

FBA, pFBA, FVA, loopless FBA, CycleFreeFlux, SteadyCom and GIMME, with knockouts and medium edited on the map.

Export

Maps as SVG or PNG, fluxes as CSV, models as SBML or COBRA JSON, and a reproducibility bundle with checksums.

Privacy

Every calculation runs in your browser. Models, maps and results are not uploaded anywhere. The app keeps your current work in this browser's storage so that it survives a reload. There is no login, no cookie and no tracking. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Offline and desktop

Download the app as a single HTML file (18 MB) and open it with no internet connection. A Windows desktop version with HiGHS, and optionally Gurobi or CPLEX, is in the repository. Tested in Chromium and Firefox.
